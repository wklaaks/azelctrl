I used these plastic spacers to mount the NODEMCU DRIVER BOARD and the ADS1115 board. 
You have to trim the plastic past somewhat to make them go through the small mounting holes. 
There are probably better options out there, but this is what I had and used. 
They pop through the board on the side where you trim back the plastic just enough to fit throug the hole without too much pressure. 
Then the other side of these plastic bases, you glue it either directly into the plastic box you are using, or onto another plastic base, which fits snuggly into the box.
I used this approach so that should I have to remove and swap out any of the boards, that I can simply lift the insides up and out of the box, because you probably will have to cut the spacers to
